        Rat Game
        
Field guide

        

          Get onto the table. Leave your hole and head left. Jump onto
          the mint stool from its side. Walk to the ladder, then hold Climb to
          reach the pink tablecloth.
        

        

          Fill up and steal. Use Action beside the small tasting portions
          until your belly is full. Take the whole pastry on the other plate.
          Come back to the hole on the right and use Action to stash it.
        

        

          Lose the chef. Hide with Action inside the teapot, under a
          table or at home. If he sees you hide, he can search there. Knock the
          spoon to draw him away. Boost spends belly energy.
        

        

          A / D or ← / → to walk · Space to jump · W / ↑ to climb · E for Action
          · Shift to boost · Esc to pause
