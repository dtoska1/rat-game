        Rat Game
        
Field guide

        

          Get onto the table. Leave your hole and head left. Jump onto
          the mint stool from beside or beneath it. On a keyboard use Space, W
          or ↑ to jump. At a ladder, tap the same Jump button to climb to the top,
          or hold W / ↑ on a keyboard.
        

        

          Fill up and steal. Use Action beside the small tasting portions
          until your belly is full. Take the whole pastry on the other plate.
          Come back to the hole on the right and use Action to stash it.
        

        
Hard Chaos challenge. Two faster mop robots patrol the kitchen, and floor steam vents glow amber before blasting red-hot steam. Watch the timing, jump over the jets, or hide in cover. The chef becomes more aggressive with each cheese you steal. The MOP, PUFF and QUACK pranks provide shorter windows, and the final escape takes 38 seconds instead of 50.

        
Two ways to play. Choose Play HARD Chaos on the title screen to start with the mop bot, prank switches and cheese chase immediately. Choose Original story to play all three pastry deliveries first and then unlock the chaos round.

        

          Secret cheese round. After stashing all three pastries,
          discover three vintage cheese wedges: one near the spoon, one on a
          tabletop, and one in the far-left aisle. Pocket each using Action.
          A little mop robot roams the floor. Jump over it, or activate the
          green MOP breaker to shut it down for 15 seconds. Prank the chef by
          using two of the green MOP, pink PUFF and yellow QUACK devices.
          PUFF blinds the chef for eight seconds. QUACK lures him away and
          restores energy. Collect all the cheeses, prank him twice and get home.
        

        
Mischief notebook. Each heist chooses three optional silly challenges. Completing them earns more points, but you can finish the story without them. Your current list: Start to see your pranks.

        
Squeak (Q / touch button). Lure the chef to your current location. It costs 8 energy and has a 14-second cooldown. Use it to set up a distraction, but do not squeak if you cannot escape!

        
Final escape. Once you have all three cheeses and have sabotaged at least two gadgets, the kitchen alarm starts. Get home within 50 seconds or the chef wins.

        

          Lose the chef. Hide with Action inside the teapot, under a
          table or at home. If he sees you hide, he can search there. Knock the
          spoon to draw him away. If he reaches for you on the floor, jump his grab.
          Boost spends belly energy.
        

        

          A / D or ← / → to walk · Space, W or ↑ to jump · W / ↑ at a ladder to climb · E for Action
          · Shift to boost · Q to squeak · Esc to pause
